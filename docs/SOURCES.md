# 图片来源与处理说明

> 本文记录各国家/发行地区的首选来源及特殊拆分、裁切和版本选择方式。逐条来源 URL、原图路径与质量参数以 `currencies.json` 和 `collection_log.json` 为准。

Numista pages were accessed through the in-app browser because direct command-line requests receive Cloudflare challenge pages. Numista original images were downloaded from the page's linked `original.jpg` assets, then normalized locally. Canada banknote images were downloaded from Bank of Canada official security-features pages.

Source attribution and original image URLs are preserved in `currencies.json` and `collection_log.json`.

Norway images were downloaded from Norges Bank legal tender denomination pages.
Sweden images were downloaded from Sveriges Riksbank valid banknote and valid coin pages. Swedish coin source images contain both sides in one official image; the dataset crops these at the horizontal midpoint into front/back originals before normalization.
Switzerland banknote images were downloaded from the Swiss National Bank ninth-series page. Switzerland coin images were downloaded from Swissmint's circulation-coins page. Swissmint coin source images contain both sides in one official image; the dataset crops these at the horizontal midpoint into front/back originals before normalization.
Philippines images were downloaded from Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas New Generation Currency and First Philippine Polymer Banknote pages. The NGC coin source image contains all current denominations in one transparent official image; the dataset crops the selected latest per-denomination obverse/reverse regions before normalization.
Denmark images were downloaded from Danmarks Nationalbank current banknote/coin pages and press photos. Danish banknote and coin source images contain both sides in one official image; the dataset splits these into front/back originals before normalization.
Chile images were downloaded from Banco Central de Chile's Billetes y Monedas image index. The 1- and 5-peso coins were omitted because they were withdrawn from circulation in 2017.
Costa Rica images were downloaded from Banco Central de Costa Rica's current banknote and coin pages. The official coin page lists multiple current circulating reverse designs for 25, 50, and 500 colones; each listed `corriente` design is included as a separate denomination/design entry.
Uruguay images were downloaded from Banco Central del Uruguay's banknote and coin subsites. Uruguay banknote source images contain both sides in one official display image; the dataset splits the upper and lower halves into front/back originals before normalization. Uruguay coin entries use the official current coin page display images.
United Arab Emirates images were downloaded from Central Bank of the UAE circulated-currency pages. Banknotes were split from official combined front/back images, using only the latest issue year per denomination; AED 20 and AED 200 official images were cropped around the latest banknote views to exclude page issue-year labels. Coins were downloaded from official per-side PNG images.
Bahrain images were downloaded from Central Bank of Bahrain currency pages. Banknotes use the 2023 upgraded fourth issue front/back images. Coins use the CBB Current Coins page front/back images; WordPress original image URLs were used where the page displayed thumbnail variants.
Kuwait images were downloaded from Central Bank of Kuwait banknote and coin pages. Banknotes use sixth-issue large front/back images released into circulation on 29 June 2014. Coins use official current coin 3x combined images, split vertically into front/back originals before normalization.
Georgia images were downloaded from National Bank of Georgia banknote and coin pages. Banknotes use the current upgraded series listed under banknotes issued since 2016. Coins use NBG circulation-coin front/back image files referenced in the page data, including the two listed 10-lari designs.
Taiwan images were downloaded from the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) Currency Museum English circulation APIs. Banknotes use `/server/web/circulation_en/52`; coins use `/server/web/circulation_en/54`; detail records provide front/back PNG filenames under `/ExhibitsImg/Search/`. The museum lists two current 10-dollar coin designs, both included as separate entries.
Ukraine images were downloaded from National Bank of Ukraine current banknote and coin pages. Fourth-generation current banknotes of 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, and 1,000 hryvnias are included; 1-, 2-, 5-, and 10-hryvnia banknotes ceased to be legal tender on 2 March 2026 and are omitted. Current hryvnia coins are included, and 1-, 2-, 5-, 10-, and 25-kopiika coins are included because the NBU states those small-denomination coins remain legal tender, with 10-kopiika coins being gradually withdrawn from circulation from 1 October 2025.
Morocco images were downloaded from Bank Al-Maghrib 2023-series banknote and coin pages. Banknotes use official separate front/back reference images. Coins use official horizontal obverse/reverse composite images; the dataset splits the composites vertically into front/back originals before trimming white border and normalizing dimensions.
Qatar banknote images were downloaded from Qatar Central Bank's official Current Banknotes page. All seven listed denominations use separate official front/back PNGs; the 1-, 5-, 10-, and 50-riyal entries use the updated 2026 images. Qatar coins remain uncollected under the partial-coverage policy because a comparable official current-circulation front/back set was not verified.
Brunei banknote images were downloaded from Brunei Darussalam Central Bank's official Currency in Circulation page. The five 2025-family denominations use clean separate front/back PNGs; the official announcement dated 2025-09-22 scheduled their circulation for the first half of 2026, and the current circulation page now lists them. The security-features versions were rejected because they contain green annotation markers. Coins remain uncollected because the latest official 2017 coin image contains visible `SPECIMEN` overlays.

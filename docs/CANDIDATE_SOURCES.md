# 候选来源调研记录

> 以下国家/币种曾进行来源调研，但尚未满足当前完整正反面、清晰度或可复现性要求。保留记录用于避免重复调查；条件变化后可重新评估。

- United Kingdom / GBP was investigated on 2026-07-04 and rechecked on 2026-07-13 under the partial-coverage policy. Bank of England provides complete King Charles III banknote front/back images for 5, 10, 20, and 50 pounds, but the largest official files remain only about 570x296-301 even when larger CMS width parameters are requested. Royal Mint pages provide high-quality reverse images for the 2023 definitive coins, but not complete per-denomination obverse images. GBP therefore remains uncollected under the high-resolution rule.
- South Korea / KRW was investigated on 2026-07-04. Bank of Korea provides complete current banknote and coin pages, but the public images found on those pages were low-resolution examples (banknotes around 301x132, coins around 99x100). KRW was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- New Zealand / NZD was investigated on 2026-07-04 and rechecked on 2026-07-05. Reserve Bank of New Zealand current banknote/coin pages were previously blocked from command-line access by Cloudflare. During the 2026-07-05 recheck, current coin pages exposed reverse-side denomination images, but complete obverse images were not available from the same source. NZD was not added under the complete front/back rule.
- Czechia / CZK was investigated on 2026-07-04. Czech National Bank provides complete current banknote and coin pages, but the public images found on those pages were low-resolution examples (banknotes around 310x153, coin combined images around 350x165). CZK was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Malaysia / MYR was investigated on 2026-07-04. Bank Negara Malaysia currency pages returned CloudFront errors from command-line access, so MYR was not added pending browser-based or alternate-source collection.
- South Africa / ZAR was investigated on 2026-07-04. South African Reserve Bank banknote and coin pages rejected command-line access, so ZAR was not added pending browser-based or alternate-source collection.
- Israel / ILS was investigated on 2026-07-05. Bank of Israel coin pages expose several high-resolution current coin images, but complete high-resolution current banknote front/back image URLs were not fully identified from the page assets during this pass. ILS was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Peru / PEN was investigated on 2026-07-05. Banco Central de Reserva del Peru image URLs were identified for current banknotes, but the public images found were low-resolution examples around 246x119; complete high-resolution current coin images were not identified in this pass. PEN was not added under the current quality and complete-country rules.
- Colombia / COP was investigated on 2026-07-05. Banco de la Republica pages expose official circulation banknote and coin pages, but complete high-quality front/back image URLs for all current denominations were not fully identified from command-line retrieval during this pass. COP was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Mexico / MXN was investigated on 2026-07-05. Banco de Mexico provides high-quality downloadable PDF materials for current banknotes, and official current coin pages expose complete obverse/reverse imagery for several denominations, but some current coin denominations such as 5 centavos and 10 pesos only exposed low-resolution web PNGs during this pass. MXN was not added under the current quality and complete-country rules.
- United States / USD was investigated on 2026-07-05. U.S. Currency Education Program denomination pages expose official current-note feature PDFs whose embedded images can be extracted, but the extracted note images include large `SPECIMEN` overlays and reverse images are much lower resolution than the obverse images. U.S. Mint circulating coin image-library pages were also investigated, but direct command-line access to the image-library page returned 403 during this pass. USD was not added under the current high-quality and reproducible-source rules.
- Trinidad and Tobago / TTD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago current banknote and coin pages expose complete front/back display images for current polymer notes and coins, but the original public images found during this pass were web-resolution examples around 700x294 for notes and 626x258 for coins. TTD was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Romania / RON was investigated on 2026-07-05. Banca Nationala a Romaniei current banknote and coin pages were accessible, but the static HTML did not expose denomination image URLs; the content appears to be loaded through a dynamic path not identified during this pass. RON was not added under the reproducible complete-source rule.
- Poland / PLN was investigated on 2026-07-05. Narodowy Bank Polski current banknote and coin pages returned 403 to direct command-line retrieval during this pass. PLN was not added pending browser-based collection or another reproducible official source.
- Brazil / BRL was investigated on 2026-07-05. Banco Central do Brasil cédulas e moedas pages returned only a small frontend application shell to direct command-line retrieval, without exposed denomination image URLs. BRL was not added pending browser/API discovery of complete high-quality front/back images.
- Eastern Caribbean dollar / XCD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Eastern Caribbean Central Bank official pages describe current polymer notes and coins, but the pages returned 403 to direct command-line retrieval during this pass. XCD was not added pending browser-based collection and a country/issuer modeling decision for the multi-country currency area.
- Hungary / HUF was investigated on 2026-07-05. Magyar Nemzeti Bank's official `Forint banknotes and coins` PDF was downloadable and exposes complete current denomination imagery, but embedded image sizes found during this pass were low-resolution examples, with banknote images around 434x197 and coin images around 100 px. HUF was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Armenia / AMD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Armenia's current banknote pages list the third-series banknotes and provide official combined front/back images and PDFs, but the public page images are combined low-to-moderate-resolution examples and the PDF route would require denomination-specific rendering/cropping that was not completed in this pass. The official coins page exposes second-series coin images around 201x201 and does not provide complete high-resolution front/back coin images. AMD was not added under the current high-resolution and reproducible complete-source rules.
- Sri Lanka / LKR was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Sri Lanka's current note and coin pages expose complete front/back images, but the public banknote images are low-resolution examples around 428-512 px wide and the current coin images are combined web images around 663-729 px wide. LKR was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Maldives / MVR was investigated on 2026-07-05 and rechecked on 2026-07-07. Maldives Monetary Authority's current-circulation template exposes complete banknote and coin image paths, but the latest 2026 20- and 100-rufiyaa banknote front/back images carry large `SPECIMEN` overlays. During the 2026-07-07 recheck, the official Angular routes and JSON files were still reproducible, but direct downloads of large official note images repeatedly timed out before completion. MVR was not added under the clean latest-issue image and reproducible-download rules.
- Jordan / JOD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Jordan currency pages repeatedly timed out during direct SSL retrieval in this pass, so complete reproducible image URLs could not be verified. JOD was not added pending browser-based collection or another stable official endpoint.
- Fiji / FJD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Reserve Bank of Fiji provides high-resolution 2012-series banknote images and coin material, but the latest new polymer banknotes unveiled in December 2025 and released in January 2026 were only found as press-release photos/videos during this pass, not complete per-denomination front/back images. FJD was not added under the latest-issue rule.
- Seychelles / SCR was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Seychelles' current family page exposes only two combined landing images for all notes and all coins, not reproducible per-denomination front/back images. SCR was not added under the complete per-denomination source rule.
- Jamaica / JMD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Bank of Jamaica's new 2023 banknote page exposes campaign/thumbnail imagery rather than per-denomination front/back images, and the coin page exposes only larger-denomination coin images. JMD was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Barbados / BBD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Barbados' circulation coin page exposes high-quality current coin images, but the 2022 polymer banknote pages expose portraits and security-feature details rather than complete front/back banknote images. BBD was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Albania / ALL was investigated on 2026-07-05. Bank of Albania's current banknote and coin pages expose official image URLs, but the clean current 2000-leke banknote images available from the detail page are only about 413x204, while the higher-resolution gallery route includes inconsistent/low-resolution or specimen-named assets. ALL was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- North Macedonia / MKD was investigated on 2026-07-05. The National Bank currency page returned 403 to direct command-line retrieval during this pass. MKD was not added pending browser-based collection or another reproducible official source.
- Indonesia / IDR was investigated on 2026-07-05 and rechecked on 2026-07-13 under the partial-coverage policy. Bank Indonesia's official currency page confirms seven TE 2022 banknote denominations and exposes complete front/back JPGs around 1324-1611 px wide, but visual inspection during the recheck found a large red `SPESIMEN` overlay across both sides of every denomination. Current TE 2016 coin obverse PNGs are available at 1350x1350 for the main denominations, while clean matching reverse images were not confirmed and reverse-facing 3D-atlas frames also carry specimen overlays. IDR remains uncollected under the clean-image rule.
- Turkey / TRY was investigated on 2026-07-05. The Central Bank of the Republic of Turkiye banknote pages list current/latest banknote issue information, but Turkish Mint circulating-coin pages were rejected by the ministry WAF during direct retrieval. TRY was not added under the reproducible complete-country rule.
- Serbia / RSD was investigated on 2026-07-05. National Bank of Serbia currency pages were identified, but direct retrieval of the banknote and coin pages was blocked by the site for technical/security reasons. RSD was not added pending browser-based collection or another stable official endpoint.
- Moldova / MDL was investigated on 2026-07-05. National Bank of Moldova banknote pages expose clean front/back images around 1100x584 for current notes, but current coin pages expose only small GIF images around 190x190. MDL was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Iceland / ISK was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of Iceland valid banknote and valid coin pages are Blazor-rendered shells in direct retrieval, and the underlying image asset/API paths were not identified during this pass. ISK was not added under the reproducible complete-source rule.
- Bahamas / BSD was investigated on 2026-07-05. Central Bank of The Bahamas exposes current banknote and coin pages; coin images are available as combined front/back display images, but latest CRISP Evolution banknote images include specimen-named/specimen-style assets for several denominations. BSD was not added under the clean latest-issue image rule.
- Bosnia and Herzegovina / BAM was investigated on 2026-07-05. The Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina exposes complete current KM coin front/back PNGs around 424-718 px and complete current/latest banknote front/back JPGs, including 2024 and 2022 issues. The banknote source images are low-resolution examples around 369-391 px wide for several denominations. BAM was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Aruba / AWG was investigated on 2026-07-05. Centrale Bank van Aruba provides 2019-series banknote downloads and confirms the 2019 banknotes replaced the 2003 series, but the current coin page provides text descriptions without complete front/back coin images. AWG was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Botswana / BWP was investigated on 2026-07-06. Bank of Botswana's official notes-and-coins page exposes complete current banknote front/back images for P10, P20, P50, P100, and P200, but the public image files are low-resolution examples around 428 px wide. The official 2013 coin PDF provides clean large reverse views and smaller obverse views for all seven current coin denominations, but the banknote quality fails the current high-resolution rule. BWP was not added.
- Belize / BZD was investigated on 2026-07-06. Central Bank of Belize's official coin page exposes complete current coin front/back PNG files, but the 2025 banknote page exposes independent front images under `specimen-notes` with visible `SPECIMEN` overlays, and the official labeled 2025 banknote PDF contains complete front/back images with explanatory arrows over the notes. BZD was not added under the clean latest-issue image rule.
- Eswatini / SZL was investigated on 2026-07-06. Central Bank of Eswatini's currency page was accessible, but the static HTML and WordPress JSON response did not expose complete current banknote and coin image URLs during this pass; the page content appears to be assembled by theme/plugin assets not identified from direct retrieval. SZL was not added under the reproducible complete-source rule.
- Thailand / THB was investigated on 2026-07-07. Bank of Thailand's current-banknotes page is accessible and exposes current series image paths, including polymer 20, 50, and 100 baht front images and a combined current-series image. Complete per-denomination banknote back images were not identified from the page assets during this pass. The official Treasury Department circulation-coin page could not be retrieved reliably from direct command-line access during this pass, so complete current coin front/back images were not verified. THB was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Guyana / GYD was investigated on 2026-07-08. Bank of Guyana's official website returned a maintenance/recovery page during direct retrieval, so current official banknote and coin image pages could not be accessed or verified. GYD was not added pending restoration of the official site or another reproducible official source.
- Oman / OMR was investigated on 2026-07-08. Central Bank of Oman's official currency page is reproducible from command-line access and confirms the current circulating denominations: 50, 20, 10, 5, 1, 1/2 rial and 100 baisa banknotes, plus 50, 25, 10, and 5 baisa coins. Its official sixth-issue security-features PDF contains complete 2020 banknote front/back images, but complete clean current coin front/back image sources were not identified during this pass. OMR was not added under the complete-country rule.
- Saudi Arabia / SAR was investigated on 2026-07-08 using the rendered Saudi Central Bank browser page because direct command-line retrieval from `sama.gov.sa` returned empty server replies. SAMA's sixth-issue page confirms the current sixth-issue banknote and coin denominations and exposes complete banknote and coin display images. The coin images are clean but low-to-moderate resolution, while the complete banknote front/back display images contain visible `SPECIMEN` overlays. The sixth-issue security-features page exposes cleaner banknote front images around 542x232 but not complete clean front/back images for every current denomination. SAR was not added under the clean complete front/back image rule.
- Bulgaria / BGN was investigated on 2026-07-09. Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026, and the lev/euro cash dual-circulation period ended on 31 January 2026; from 1 February 2026 the euro is the sole legal tender in Bulgaria. BGN was therefore not added as a current fiat currency. Bulgaria's current cash would require the unresolved multi-country EUR modeling decision, so it was skipped in this dataset pass.
- Tajikistan / TJS was investigated on 2026-07-09. National Bank of Tajikistan official banknote and coin pages are reproducible from command-line access and expose complete front/back image paths. However, the latest 2017-2018 somoni banknote images are only about 340x150 for most denominations, and the official coin images are low-resolution examples around 100-173 px. TJS was not added under the current high-resolution rule.
- Kazakhstan / KZT was investigated on 2026-07-09. National Bank of Kazakhstan official pages expose current banknote detail pages, including the 2023-2026 `Saka style` release schedule, and current circulation coin front/back images. The latest 500-tenge Saka image downloaded from the official page is high enough resolution but has a prominent red `ҮЛГІ` specimen/sample overlay across the banknote; other latest banknote images include specimen/sample filenames or lower-resolution display images, and official coin images exposed from the circulation coin catalog are around 238x205 to 250x250. KZT was not added under the clean latest-issue image and high-resolution rules.
- Argentina / ARS was investigated on 2026-07-13. Banco Central de la República Argentina's official `Billetes y monedas en vigencia` index is reproducible and currently lists 21 legal-tender banknote issues/designs and 11 legal-tender coin issues/designs, including multiple still-valid variants of the same denomination. Official coin detail pages expose usable combined obverse/reverse images, but the banknote detail pages provide an inconsistent mix of annotated security-feature sheets and combined web images rather than country-complete high-resolution isolated sides. For example, the official 2016 10-peso sheet is 1200x944 with each note view only about 700 px wide after cropping, while the official legacy 20-, 50-, and 100-peso combined images are only 800x762 for both sides together; the 20,000-peso sheet is larger but has extensive explanatory callouts. ARS was not added under the complete high-resolution and consistent reproducible-source rules.
- Dominican Republic / DOP was investigated on 2026-07-13. Banco Central de la República Dominicana's official `Billetes en circulación` page lists current 50-, 100-, 200-, 500-, 1,000-, and 2,000-peso issues, while its official `Monedas` page lists 1-, 5-, 10-, and 25-peso circulation coins. The latest banknote front files exposed by the page are only 650x275, and corresponding latest-year reverse filenames for the sampled 2023 and 2024 issues return 404; several modal records on the page likewise expose only the front image. The four official coin images are only 227x227 and show one side rather than complete obverse/reverse pairs. DOP was therefore not added under the complete front/back and high-resolution rules.
